Part B – Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist
Do’s
* Use respectful and polite language in all interactions 
* Keep your profile professional and appropriate 
* Verify information before sharing 
* Protect your personal information and privacy settings 
* Use social media to learn, network, and grow professionally 

 Don’ts
* Do not share sensitive personal or financial information 
* Avoid posting offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content 
* Do not spread fake news or unverified information 
* Avoid oversharing personal life details publicly 
* Do not engage in online arguments or cyberbullying
