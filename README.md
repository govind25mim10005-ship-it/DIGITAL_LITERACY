# DIGITAL_LITERACY
This project is based on diogital literacy by task.
Module 1: Practical Skills in Presentation Design
This module develops the ability to communicate ideas effectively through digital presentations. It focuses not just on using tools like PowerPoint or Google Slides, but on design thinking—how to present information in a structured, engaging, and visually balanced way. Students learn to use layouts, typography, color combinations, icons, and multimedia elements to make presentations impactful. It also highlights the importance of storytelling, audience understanding, and avoiding clutter to ensure clarity and professionalism during presentations.

Module 2: Creating a Strong Digital Portfolio
This module emphasizes building a professional identity in the digital world through a well-organized portfolio. It guides students on how to present their academic work, projects, skills, and achievements in a structured and appealing format. The focus is on authenticity, clarity, and relevance of content. It also introduces the idea of personal branding—how a digital portfolio can reflect personality, strengths, and career goals, making it a powerful tool for internships, higher education, and job opportunities.

Module 3: Learning Through Programming & Collaboration Platforms
This module explores modern learning environments where students can code, practice, and collaborate. It introduces platforms like Kaggle for hands-on experience in data science, coding challenges, and real-world problem solving. Along with technical skills, it highlights the importance of collaboration using tools such as shared documents, repositories, and discussion forums. The module encourages peer learning, idea exchange, and developing problem-solving skills in a connected digital ecosystem.

Module 4: Professional Communication & Online Behaviour
This module focuses on building a responsible and professional digital presence. It teaches structured email writing, including proper greetings, subject lines, concise content, and respectful tone. In addition, it covers responsible use of social media—how to communicate respectfully, avoid misinformation, maintain privacy, and represent oneself positively online. The module stresses that digital communication reflects personality and can impact academic and career opportunities.

Module 5: Cyber Safety & Financial Security Awareness
This module highlights the risks present in the digital world and how to stay protected. It explains common cyber threats such as phishing, identity theft, fake links, and online frauds. Special attention is given to financial safety, including secure use of UPI, protecting OTPs, and recognizing scam tactics. It also promotes safe practices like strong passwords, two-factor authentication, and cautious browsing. The module encourages awareness and responsible action, including reporting cyber crimes through official platforms like the National Cyber Crime Portal and helpline 1930.
